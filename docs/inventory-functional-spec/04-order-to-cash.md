# Order-to-Cash — Estimation through Customer Receipt (+ POS Billing)

Author's note: like the sibling documents in this set, this was produced by walking the actual live frontend config (`inventory-screen.model.ts`), the actual C# service (`SalesTransactionsDataService.cs`), and the actual latest `CREATE OR REPLACE` definitions in `Database/Migrations/inventory/*.sql` — not from a spec. Three of the eleven screens covered here turned out to have no real backend at all; that is reported plainly below rather than smoothed over.

## 1. The end-to-end flow, narrated

**Pre-sales (no stock, no GL, all optional):** A customer relationship usually starts as a **Sales Enquiry** (a walk-in, phone call, or website lead — captured with expected products and delivery date) or an **Estimation** (a rough costed proposal). An Estimation can be worked up into a **Proforma Invoice** — a formal pre-bill sent to the customer before any commitment (the two are genuinely linked: `inv_proforma_invoices.estimation_id` is a real foreign key into `inv_estimations`). Separately, a **Sales Quotation** is a formal priced offer with a validity window; the model layer's narrative claims "approved quotation converts to Sales Order," but this is **not backed by any database reference** — there is no `quotation_id`/`sq_id` column anywhere on `inv_sales_orders`, and Sales Quotation's own `enquiryRef` field is a plain free-text box, not a picker. Sales Enquiry itself never even reaches this far: it's a frontend-only screen with a static demo grid and a Save button that does nothing (see §2, Sales Enquiry).

**Commitment:** A **Sales Order (SO)** is the first document that represents a real customer commitment — customer, delivery date, payment terms, warehouse. It does **not** move stock or place any hold on `inv_stock_balance` on its own; it only carries `qty` per line, later compared against two bookkeeping columns (`delivered_qty`, `invoiced_qty`) that the downstream documents update. In other words, "SO reserves stock" (the copy shown in the UI) is aspirational — the actual reservation is soft/advisory, computed live at reference-pick time, not a hard quantity hold.

**Dispatch:** A **Delivery Challan (DC)** is what actually moves physical goods out of the warehouse/branch — `fn_post_delivery_challan_dispatch` decrements `inv_stock_balance` and consumes FIFO/LIFO/Weighted-Average cost layers the moment a DC is saved with `status = 'posted'`. A DC can reference an SO (per line, via `so_item_id`), be created directly with no SO, or (a flow this codebase calls "flow E") be generated *after* a Sales Invoice already exists, purely for paperwork (`si_item_id` on the DC line, no additional qty/stock effect since the SI already moved the stock in that flow). One Sales Invoice can also consolidate multiple DCs at the line level.

**Billing:** A **Sales Invoice (SI)** books the financial receivable. Migration `057_delivery_challan.sql`'s own header comment enumerates every supported flow explicitly: **SO→DC→SI**, **SO→SI direct** (no DC at all), **direct DC→SI**, **fully direct SI** (no SO, no DC), **SI-first-then-DC** (documentation only), and **one SI consolidating multiple DCs**. So yes — there is a genuine **direct-SI path**: an SI needs neither an SO nor a DC reference to be created and posted, and it moves stock itself in that case (`fn_post_sales_invoice_stock`'s `dc_item_id IS NULL` branch). When a Sales Invoice *is* saved this way (no `so_id`, no line carrying a `dc_item_id`), the UI proactively asks the user a 3-way question — "Only Invoice" / "Invoice with DC" (auto-creates and posts a DC alongside) / "Separate DC" (auto-creates a draft DC for later review) — since a direct SI otherwise leaves no paper trail of the physical dispatch.

Whether an SI carries a DC reference or not, **posting it is what creates the actual accounting entry** (`accounts.sp_post_sales_invoice`, called from C# right after the inventory-side save succeeds) — Sundry Debtors debited, category-wise income credited, CGST+SGST or IGST credited depending on interstate/intrastate classification.

**Reversal:** A **Sales Return** is raised against a posted Sales Invoice (or, less commonly, directly). Posting it increments `inv_stock_balance` back (`fn_post_sales_return_stock`) and fires `accounts.sp_reverse_sales_invoice_posting` — a real reversal journal entry, debiting the category income account and crediting Sundry Debtors. A **Credit Note** is the *financial* settlement document layered on top of a Sales Return (or directly on an invoice) — it never touches stock (the linked Sales Return already did that), and, despite the accounting infrastructure existing since migration 091, **it never posts anything to the Accounts schema at all**. It exists purely as an inventory-side record today.

**Settlement:** **Customer Receipt** records money actually received against one or more open Sales Invoices, with allocation and payment-mode breakdown. It reduces the customer's outstanding and is the one document in this chain, besides SI/SR, that genuinely posts to `accounts.tbl_trans_total_transactions` (`accounts.sp_post_receipt_voucher`).

**The parallel fast path:** **POS / Counter Billing** exists conceptually as "a sales invoice or bill with immediate stock impact and payment entry" — a walk-in-customer, no-approval-chain shortcut meant to bypass Enquiry→Quotation→Order→DC entirely. In the current codebase it is, like Sales Enquiry, a **frontend-only stub**: real fields and a real static grid, but no backend read or write path at all.

**How references actually chain (verified in `inventory.sp_get_sales_docs_for_ref`, the procedure that feeds every "pick a reference document" dropdown):**
- **Estimation → Proforma Invoice → Sales Invoice**: real FK chain (`estimation_id`, `proforma_id`).
- **Sales Order → Delivery Challan**: `inv_delivery_challans.so_id`/`so_number` (header) + `inv_delivery_challan_items.so_item_id` (line). A posted SO only appears in the DC reference picker while it has at least one line with `qty > delivered_qty`, and is excluded once *any* posted/closed SI or DC already fully claims it (a cancelled draft reference does **not** permanently block re-referencing, by design).
- **Sales Order / Delivery Challan → Sales Invoice**: `inv_sales_invoices.so_id`/`so_number` + per-line `so_item_id`/`dc_item_id`. The SI reference picker excludes DC lines/SO lines already fully invoiced, already returned (net of quantity actually returned), or already consumed by another live DC/SI reference.
- **Sales Invoice → Sales Return**: `inv_sales_returns.invoice_id`/`invoice_number`.
- **Sales Return → Credit Note**: `inv_credit_notes.sales_return_id` (or directly `sales_invoice_id`).
- **Sales Quotation → Sales Order**: **no real reference at all** (text field only) — the one broken link in an otherwise FK-backed chain.

## Estimation

**Business purpose:** Prepare a rough, itemized cost estimate for a customer across goods, services, or capacity, before any formal quotation or commitment.
**Predecessor / successor documents:** Standalone entry point (no upstream reference). Can become a Proforma Invoice (`inv_proforma_invoices.estimation_id`/`estimation_number` are real, populated fields).
**Status lifecycle:** `draft → sent → accepted → revised → cancelled` (`ck_estimation_status`, unchanged since creation).
**Key business rules:** No stock impact whatsoever — pure quoting document. No warehouse/branch field exists on this table at all (never touched by the "Full Warehouse/Branch Independence" project — it predates and was never revisited). Line items carry `gst_rate`/`discount_pct`/`amount` but no `gst_inclusive`/`taxable_amount` split (the newer pattern Sales Invoice got in migration 126 was never backported here).
**Accounting/GL impact:** None. No accounts procedure is called anywhere in the save path.
**Frontend:** `estimationConfig` → `src/app/inventory/Inventory_Transactions/estimation/estimation.ts` (thin `InventoryScreenShell` subclass, no screen-specific overrides).
**Backend — tables:** `inventory.inv_estimations` (header: `doc_number`, `doc_date`, `valid_till`, `segment_id/name`, `customer_id/name`, `reference_no`, `payment_terms`, `status`); `inventory.inv_estimation_items` (line: `product_id/name/code`, `variant_id/name`, `uom_id/name`, `attribute_name/value`, `qty`, `rate`, `discount_pct`, `gst_rate`, `amount`).
**Backend — stored procedures:** `inventory.sp_get_estimations` (list + nested items JSON); `inventory.sp_save_estimation` (upsert header, replace-all items); `inventory.sp_cancel_sales_doc` (`doc_type='estimation'` branch — flips status only, no side effects). All three are byte-for-byte unchanged since migration `032_sales_transactions.sql` — no later migration has ever touched this screen's backend.
**Known gaps or flags:** Frozen since original build — no branch/warehouse concept, no GST-inclusive pricing, no serial/batch fields, no discount validation beyond a raw percentage. Functionally complete for its narrow purpose (a costed proposal) but has not kept pace with any of the platform-wide improvements (branch independence, GST interstate posting, UOM conversion fixes) that landed on Sales Order/DC/SI/SR.

## Proforma Invoice

**Business purpose:** Issue a formal pre-bill to the customer — often required before an advance payment or a letter of credit — without creating any financial receivable or moving stock.
**Predecessor / successor documents:** Can originate from an Estimation (`estimation_id`/`estimation_number`, both real, populated on save) or be created directly. Feeds Sales Invoice: `inv_sales_invoices.proforma_id`/`proforma_number` are real, populated columns, and the C# `SaveSalesInvoiceAsync` accepts them directly from the request.
**Status lifecycle:** `draft → sent → accepted → converted → cancelled` (`ck_proforma_status`).
**Key business rules:** No stock impact — explicitly documented in the UI copy ("...without stock impact until converted"). Like Estimation, carries no warehouse/branch column and no GST-inclusive/taxable-amount split.
**Accounting/GL impact:** None.
**Frontend:** `proformaInvoiceConfig` → `src/app/inventory/Inventory_Transactions/proforma-invoice/proforma-invoice.ts` (thin shell subclass).
**Backend — tables:** `inventory.inv_proforma_invoices` (header, includes `estimation_id`/`estimation_number`, `place_of_supply`); `inventory.inv_proforma_invoice_items` (same shape as Estimation's items).
**Backend — stored procedures:** `inventory.sp_get_proforma_invoices`; `inventory.sp_save_proforma_invoice`; `inventory.sp_cancel_sales_doc` (`doc_type='proforma'`). Also unchanged since migration `032_sales_transactions.sql`.
**Known gaps or flags:** Same "frozen since 032" gap as Estimation — no branch scoping, no GST-inclusive handling. The `converted` status value exists in the CHECK constraint but nothing in the codebase ever sets it automatically when a Sales Invoice references the proforma; it would need to be set manually if used at all.

## Sales Enquiry

**Business purpose (as designed):** Capture a customer's initial requirement — products of interest, expected quantities/price, expected delivery date, source (walk-in/phone/email/website/referral/exhibition) — as the very first pipeline touchpoint, meant to convert into a Sales Quotation.
**Predecessor / successor documents:** None, in practice — see Known gaps.
**Status lifecycle:** UI shows `Open`/`Quoted` on its static demo rows only; there is no real status column or transition logic anywhere.
**Key business rules:** No stock impact, no GL impact — by design, this is meant to be the lightest possible capture screen.
**Accounting/GL impact:** None.
**Frontend:** `salesEnquiryConfig` → `src/app/inventory/Inventory_Transactions/sales-enquiry/sales-enquiry.ts` (thin shell subclass, 19 lines, identical shape to every other thin subclass — nothing hints from the frontend alone that this one is unwired).
**Backend — tables:** **None.** No `inv_sales_enquiries` (or similarly named) table exists anywhere in `Database/Migrations/inventory/`.
**Backend — stored procedures:** **None.** `SalesTransactionsDataService.cs` has no enquiry-related method; grepping the entire backend for `SalesEnquiry`/`sales_enquiry` returns zero hits outside the screen-registry row (`INV_T_SALES_ENQUIRY`, just a menu/route entry).
**Known gaps or flags:** **This is a non-functional stub, not a partially-built feature.** In `inventory-screen-shell.ts`'s central get/save dispatch (the `switch (this.config?.key)` blocks around lines 14244 and 14768), Sales Enquiry falls through to `default: return;` on load and `default: this.isSaving.set(false); return;` on save — meaning the grid always shows the same hardcoded demo rows from `inventory-screen.model.ts`, and clicking Save silently does nothing (no error, no success message, no network call). Anyone building the Enquiry→Quotation link needs to build this screen's entire backend from scratch first.

## Sales Quotation

**Business purpose:** Send a formal, priced offer to the customer with a validity window and price-list reference, ahead of a committed Sales Order.
**Predecessor / successor documents:** `enquiryRef` field exists on the form but is a **plain free-text box**, not a reference picker — there is no real link to Sales Enquiry. Narrated as converting to Sales Order, but **no FK or soft-reference column exists anywhere on `inv_sales_orders` pointing back to a quotation** — confirmed by grepping every migration for `quotation_id`/`sq_id` outside the quotation's own tables. If a business process relies on SQ→SO traceability today, it is entirely manual (matching by document number in remarks, at best).
**Status lifecycle:** `draft → sent → accepted → revised → cancelled` (`ck_sq_status`).
**Key business rules:** No stock impact. No branch/warehouse column. No GST-inclusive/taxable-amount split.
**Accounting/GL impact:** None.
**Frontend:** `salesQuotationConfig` → `src/app/inventory/Inventory_Transactions/sales-quotation/sales-quotation.ts` (thin shell subclass).
**Backend — tables:** `inventory.inv_sales_quotations` (header); `inventory.inv_sales_quotation_items` (line).
**Backend — stored procedures:** `inventory.sp_get_sales_quotations`; `inventory.sp_save_sales_quotation`; `inventory.sp_cancel_sales_doc` (`doc_type='salesquotation'`). Unchanged since migration `032_sales_transactions.sql` — the only one of the three genuinely backend-wired "pre-sales" screens (unlike Sales Enquiry).
**Known gaps or flags:** The advertised Quotation→Sales Order conversion is a UI/process convention only, not a data-model guarantee — nothing prevents two different Sales Orders from being (or not being) linked back to the same quotation, and no report can trace "which quotations actually became orders" from the database alone.

## Sales Order (SO)

**Business purpose:** Formally confirm a customer's order — items, delivery date, payment terms, delivery address/warehouse — as the trigger for downstream dispatch and billing.
**Predecessor / successor documents:** Created directly (no enforced predecessor). Feeds Delivery Challan (`so_id`/`so_number` header + `so_item_id` per line) and/or Sales Invoice directly (`so_id`/`so_number` + `so_item_id` per line) — an SO can be fulfilled via DC first or invoiced straight from the SO with no DC at all.
**Status lifecycle:** `draft → posted → processing → delivered → cancelled` (`ck_so_status`; the original `confirmed` value was renamed to `posted` in migration `078_sales_order_posted_status.sql`, with existing rows migrated in place).
**Key business rules:** Despite the UI narrative ("SO reserves stock in warehouse"), **an SO never writes to `inv_stock_balance`** — confirmed by grepping every migration for any stock-balance touch keyed off `inv_sales_orders`/`so_id`; there is none. What it *does* do is carry two bookkeeping columns per line, `delivered_qty` and `invoiced_qty` (added in migration `057_delivery_challan.sql`), which the DC/SI reference-picker procedure (`sp_get_sales_docs_for_ref`) uses to compute `remaining_qty` and decide whether the SO/line is still available to reference — so the "reservation" is a soft, advisory computation at pick time, not a hard quantity hold. `warehouse_id`/`warehouse_name`/`place_of_supply` (migration 087) and `channel_partner_id`/`name` (migration 136) exist on the header. No serial/batch capture at SO stage (that happens at DC/SI time).
**Accounting/GL impact:** None. Saving/posting an SO never calls any `accounts.sp_*` procedure.
**Frontend:** `salesOrderConfig` → `src/app/inventory/Inventory_Transactions/sales-order/sales-order.ts` (shell subclass with light overrides; also has a `.no-duplicate-variant-subcell.spec.ts` guarding a specific line-grid bug fix).
**Backend — tables:** `inventory.inv_sales_orders` (header: `doc_number`, `doc_date`, `delivery_date`, `due_date`, `segment_id/name`, `customer_id/name/gstin`, `channel_partner_id/name`, `payment_terms`, `delivery_location`, `warehouse_id/name`, `place_of_supply`, `reference_no`, `status`); `inventory.inv_sales_order_items` (line: product/variant/attribute/UOM identity, `qty`, `rate`, `discount_pct`, `gst_rate`, `gst_inclusive`, `taxable_amount`, `tax_amount`, `amount`, `delivered_qty`, `invoiced_qty`).
**Backend — stored procedures:** `inventory.sp_get_sales_orders`; `inventory.sp_save_sales_order`; `inventory.sp_cancel_sales_doc` (`doc_type='salesorder'` — flips status only, no reversal needed since SO never moved anything); `inventory.sp_get_sales_docs_for_ref` (`doc_type='SO'` branch — the actual reference-availability engine described above, latest defined in migration `137_channel_partner_reference_carry.sql`).
**Known gaps or flags:** The gap between the marketed "reserves stock" behavior and the actual soft/advisory reference-availability check is real and worth calling out to anyone relying on SO as a hard stock commitment — two different SOs against the same product/warehouse can both show full remaining quantity right up until one of them actually dispatches via DC or bills directly.

## Delivery Challan (DC)

**Business purpose:** Physically dispatch goods from a warehouse/branch against a Sales Order (or directly), producing the document that travels with the shipment (vehicle, transporter, LR number).
**Predecessor / successor documents:** Optionally references a Sales Order (`so_id`/`so_number` header, `so_item_id` per line) or an already-posted Sales Invoice (`si_id`/`si_number` header, `si_item_id` per line — the "SI-first-then-DC" documentation-only flow). Feeds Sales Invoice via `dc_item_id` per SI line; a single SI can consolidate lines from multiple DCs.
**Status lifecycle:** `draft → posted → closed → cancelled` (`ck_dc_status`). `closed` is set two ways: automatically, the moment every line's `invoiced_qty` catches up to its `dispatch_qty` (inside `fn_post_sales_invoice_stock`); or manually via the explicit **Close DC** action (`sp_close_delivery_challan`, migration 138) for a DC the business has decided will never be fully invoiced. "Partially Invoiced" shown in the UI is a **derived display label**, not a stored status — computed live by `sp_get_delivery_challans` by comparing `dispatch_qty` vs `invoiced_qty`.
**Key business rules:** Posting a DC is what actually decrements physical stock (`inventory.fn_post_delivery_challan_dispatch`) — goods leave the warehouse/branch at **DC-post time, not SI-post time**. It validates dispatch qty against the SO line's remaining quantity (hard `RAISE EXCEPTION`, not a soft warning) and explicitly blocks dispatching against an SO line that was already invoiced *directly* (bypassing any DC) for more than the remaining amount — a genuine cross-check between the two fulfilment paths (migration `131_dc_dispatch_blocks_after_direct_invoice.sql`). Consumes FIFO/LIFO/Weighted-Average cost layers via `fn_consume_stock_cost_layers` (method driven by the product's own `valuation_method`, defaulting to FIFO, falling back to the product's flat `cost_price` if layers run out). Serial-tracked products get their units set to `reserved` at DC-post time (not yet `sold` — that happens at SI-post time). Supports the merged **Warehouse/Branch picker** (Full Warehouse/Branch Independence project) — `from_warehouse_id`/`from_warehouse_name` and `branch_id`/`branch_name` both exist on the header (migration 077), and as of migration `164_delivery_challan_branch_stock_posting.sql`, a branch-only pick posts stock correctly against `fn_upsert_stock_balance`'s branch-aware match key instead of silently defaulting to NULL. That same migration also upgraded DC's previous silent "clamp qty_on_hand to zero on insufficient stock" behavior into a hard refuse (`RAISE EXCEPTION 'Insufficient stock...'`).
**Accounting/GL impact:** None. DC never calls any `accounts.sp_*` procedure — it is a pure stock-movement document; the financial entry only happens later at Sales Invoice post time.
**Frontend:** `deliveryChallanConfig` → `src/app/inventory/Inventory_Transactions/delivery-challan/delivery-challan.ts` (shell subclass with light overrides; carries the same `.no-duplicate-variant-subcell.spec.ts` line-grid regression guard as Sales Order/Sales Return).
**Backend — tables:** `inventory.inv_delivery_challans` (header: `dc_number`, `dc_date`, `segment_id/name`, `so_id/number`, `si_id/number`, `customer_id/name`, `channel_partner_id/name`, `from_warehouse_id/name`, `branch_id/name`, `vehicle`, `transporter`, `lr_no`, `delivery_address`, `status`); `inventory.inv_delivery_challan_items` (line: `so_item_id`, `si_item_id`, product/variant/attribute/UOM identity, `so_qty`, `dispatch_qty`, `invoiced_qty`, `batch_serial`, `serial_numbers`).
**Backend — stored procedures:** `inventory.sp_get_delivery_challans` (list, with a `pending_invoice` filter and the derived `partially_invoiced` status label); `inventory.sp_save_delivery_challan` (upsert; folds posting into the same "status transitions to `posted`" guard idiom as GRN/PI, no separate `sp_post_*`); `inventory.fn_post_delivery_challan_dispatch` (the actual stock-decrement + SO qty-tracking + serial-reservation function, latest defined in migration 164, one of the ~10 functions protected by `inventory.trg_guard_serial_qty_functions`); `inventory.sp_close_delivery_challan` (manual close + stock reversal, migration 138); `inventory.sp_cancel_sales_doc` (`doc_type='deliverychallan'` — blocks cancelling an already-invoiced DC, otherwise reverses `delivered_qty`, stock, and serial reservations).
**Known gaps or flags:**
- **`sp_close_delivery_challan` is not branch-aware, and this is a real, still-live regression.** It reverses the unbilled remainder of a partially-invoiced DC using a hand-rolled `UPDATE inventory.inv_stock_balance ... WHERE COALESCE(warehouse_id,0) = COALESCE(v_dc.from_warehouse_id,0)` — it never reads or matches on `v_dc.branch_id` at all, even though `inv_delivery_challans.branch_id` has existed since migration 077 and the *forward* dispatch posting (`fn_post_delivery_challan_dispatch`) was made fully branch-aware in migration 164. For a branch-only DC (`from_warehouse_id IS NULL`), this reversal's `COALESCE(...,0) = COALESCE(...,0)` match will hit **any** other branch-only stock-balance row for the same product, not necessarily the correct branch's — a real cross-branch stock corruption risk on Close DC.
- **`sp_cancel_sales_doc`'s DC-cancellation branch has the identical bug** — its stock-reversal `UPDATE` also matches on `warehouse_id` only, ignoring `branch_id`, for the exact same reason (it was last touched in migration 121, before the branch-independence work landed on DC in 164).
- The originally-flagged "DC branch-field handling" issue on the *forward* stock-decrement path was confirmed to have been **fixed** in migration 164 (verified live via the migration's own `pg_get_functiondef` pre-check) — so the outstanding branch-awareness gap on this screen today is specifically in the **close** and **cancel** reversal paths, not the original post path.

## Sales Invoice

**Business purpose:** Bill the customer and book the financial receivable — the one document in this chain that both moves stock (in the direct/no-DC case) and posts to the general ledger.
**Predecessor / successor documents:** Optionally references a Sales Order (`so_id`/`so_number` header, `so_item_id` per line) and/or a Delivery Challan (`dc_item_id` per line), a Proforma Invoice (`proforma_id`/`proforma_number`), or none of the above (a fully **direct SI**). Feeds Sales Return (`invoice_id`/`invoice_number`).
**Status lifecycle:** `draft → posted → cancelled/paid` (`ck_sales_invoice_status`). A **posted** SI cannot be cancelled directly (`sp_cancel_sales_doc` raises an explicit exception: "Posted Sales Invoices cannot be cancelled directly — use Sales Return / Credit Note") — cancellation is only allowed while still in `draft`.
**Key business rules:** Posting decrements stock at the selected Warehouse/Branch **only for lines that don't already carry a `dc_item_id`** (`fn_post_sales_invoice_stock`) — a DC-linked line's stock already left the building at DC-post time, so this function correctly skips it and only rolls up the DC's own `invoiced_qty`/auto-close check instead. Direct lines (no DC, no SO) consume FIFO/LIFO/Weighted-Average cost layers the same way DC does, via `fn_consume_stock_cost_layers`. Validates invoice qty against remaining DC/SO quantity with a hard exception (not soft). Supports the merged Warehouse/Branch picker (as of migration 166, a branch-only pick posts stock correctly, and the old silent clamp-to-zero-on-insufficient-stock was upgraded to a hard refuse, same pattern as DC).
  **Interbranch Sale toggle** (a real, existing feature, off by default so no existing invoice's behavior changes unless explicitly turned on): when `interbranchSale = 'Yes'`, the invoice's Branch field becomes the actual fulfilment scope, and at save/post time the frontend (`interbranchSaleEnabled()`/`planInterbranchAutoTransfers()` in `inventory-screen-shell.ts`) checks whether every line's stock is actually available at the invoicing warehouse; if not, it looks across every warehouse under the *same* branch, and — with the user's explicit confirmation — **auto-creates and posts one or more Stock Transfers** to move the shortfall in from a sibling warehouse **before** the Sales Invoice itself saves. If a line can't be covered anywhere in the branch, the user is warned ("Stock short even across branch warehouses") and can still choose to post anyway. With the toggle off, the same over-sell case only produces a soft, non-blocking "Stock may go negative — Post anyway?" warning, exactly as before this feature existed.
  **Direct-SI auto-DC prompt** (a real, existing feature): when an SI is saved with no SO reference and no line already carrying a `dc_item_id` (i.e., a genuinely direct SI), the frontend detects this and offers a 3-way choice before finishing the save — "Only Invoice" (no follow-up), "Invoice with DC" (auto-generates and **posts** a DC from the SI's own line data immediately, printing both documents), or "Separate DC" (auto-generates the DC as a **draft** for later review/edit). Any SI created *with* an SO or DC reference skips this prompt entirely and behaves exactly as it always did.
**Accounting/GL impact:** Yes — the only inventory-side screen (besides Customer Receipt and, indirectly, Sales Return) with a real GL posting. `SaveSalesInvoiceAsync` calls `accounts.sp_post_sales_invoice` immediately after the inventory-side save succeeds, but **only when `status == 'posted'`**. Legs posted: **Sundry Debtors (1310) debited** for the full invoice total; **category-wise income account credited** (one row per distinct product category on the invoice, via `accounts.fn_ensure_party_subledger(3, category_name, 'I', ...)` — there is no single flat "Sale of Goods" head, income is split by category); **C-CGST(2049)+C-SGST(2050)** credited for an intrastate sale, or **C-IGST(2051)** credited for an interstate sale (decided by `inventory.fn_is_interstate_transaction`, comparing the invoice's own GSTIN/state to the customer's); a mirrored row also lands in `taxation.tbl_trans_gst`. **This posting is NOT branch-aware for the ledger itself**: `accounts.sp_post_sales_invoice` (latest definition, migration 166) hardcodes `v_company_code := 'COMP1'; v_branch_code := 'BNCH1'` for every row it inserts into `accounts.tbl_trans_total_transactions`, regardless of which real branch the invoice belongs to. Migration 166 *did* make the **GST interstate/intrastate classification itself** branch-aware (it now reads the invoice's real `branch_id` before falling back to `warehouse_id → inv_warehouses.branch_id`), but that only affects which tax head gets credited, not which company/branch's books the entry lands in — a subtle but real distinction worth keeping straight.
**Frontend:** `salesInvoiceConfig` → `src/app/inventory/Inventory_Transactions/sales-invoice/sales-invoice.ts` (shell subclass; the only screen-specific additions are extra shared modals for Delivery Address, Transport Details, and the compact line-level Product Picker).
**Backend — tables:** `inventory.inv_sales_invoices` (header: `doc_number`, `doc_date`, `due_date`, `segment_id/name`, `proforma_id/number`, `so_id/number`, `customer_id/name/gstin`, `channel_partner_id/name`, `place_of_supply`, `branch_id/name`, `warehouse_id/name`, `transport_mode`, `vehicle_no`, `payment_terms`, `status`); `inventory.inv_sales_invoice_items` (line: full product/variant/attribute/UOM identity, `qty`, `rate`, `mrp`, `selling_price`, `discount_pct`, `gst_rate`, `gst_inclusive`, `taxable_amount`, `tax_amount`, `amount`, `batch_no`, `serial_no`/`serial_numbers`, `so_item_id`, `dc_item_id`); `accounts.tbl_trans_total_transactions` (the GL journal rows); `taxation.tbl_trans_gst` (GST summary row per posting).
**Backend — stored procedures:** `inventory.sp_get_sales_invoices`; `inventory.sp_save_sales_invoice` (latest defined in migration 161, folding the posted-status transition guard in); `inventory.fn_post_sales_invoice_stock` (the actual stock-decrement + serial-consumption + DC/SO rollup function, latest defined in migration 166, guarded by `trg_guard_serial_qty_functions`); `accounts.sp_post_sales_invoice` (the GL posting, latest defined in migration 166); `inventory.sp_cancel_sales_doc` (`doc_type='salesinvoice'` — draft-only, as above).
**Known gaps or flags:** The hardcoded `'COMP1'`/`'BNCH1'` company/branch code on the ledger side is a known, documented gap (also called out in `07-accounting-integration.md`) — this system has never had genuinely separate per-branch accounting books for Sales Invoice, despite `branch_code` existing as a real column and despite the rest of this screen (stock posting, GST classification) being fully branch-aware.

## Sales Return

**Business purpose:** Accept goods back from a customer against a posted Sales Invoice (or directly), restoring stock and reversing the corresponding financial receivable.
**Predecessor / successor documents:** References a posted Sales Invoice (`invoice_id`/`invoice_number`); can carry a `credit_note_ref` free-text pointer, and is itself the real FK target for Credit Note (`sales_return_id`).
**Status lifecycle:** `draft → posted → cancelled`.
**Key business rules:** Posting increments `inv_stock_balance` back at the invoice's original warehouse/branch (`fn_post_sales_return_stock`) — the exact symmetric undo of the SI's own decrement, matched on warehouse/product/variant. Serial-tracked units that were `sold` are reverted (scoped specifically to the same source invoice, so a shared serial-number text on a different invoice can't be flipped back by mistake — migration `116_return_stock_transition_source_scope.sql`). Cancelling a *posted* return correctly reverses both the stock increment and the serial-status flip it made (migration `120_cancel_stock_reversal_uom_conversion.sql`/`121`).
**Accounting/GL impact:** Yes — `SaveSalesReturnAsync` calls `accounts.sp_reverse_sales_invoice_posting` when `status == 'posted'`, mirroring the Sales Invoice posting in reverse: Sundry Debtors **credited**, category income **debited**. This directly contradicts the sibling accounting-integration document's blanket claim that Sales Return "does not currently post to `accounts.tbl_trans_total_transactions` at all" — that claim was checked here against the live C# call site and the procedure's own SQL body and found to be **incorrect as of this review**; Sales Return does post a real reversal entry. It shares the same `'COMP1'`/`'BNCH1'` hardcoding as Sales Invoice's forward posting.
**Frontend:** `salesReturnConfig` → `src/app/inventory/Inventory_Transactions/sales-return/sales-return.ts` (shell subclass, same `.no-duplicate-variant-subcell.spec.ts` guard as SO/DC).
**Backend — tables:** `inventory.inv_sales_returns` (header: `return_number`, `return_date`, `segment_id/name`, `customer_id/name`, `channel_partner_id/name`, `invoice_id/number`, `credit_note_ref`, `return_to_warehouse_id/name`, `return_reason`, `subtotal`, `tax_amount`, `total_amount`, `status`); `inventory.inv_sales_return_items` (line: product/variant/attribute/UOM identity, `invoiced_qty`, `return_qty`, `rate`, `gst_rate`, `taxable_amount`, `tax_amount`, `return_amount`, `reason`, `serial_numbers`).
**Backend — stored procedures:** `inventory.sp_get_sales_returns`; `inventory.sp_save_sales_return`; `inventory.fn_post_sales_return_stock` (stock reversal); `accounts.sp_reverse_sales_invoice_posting` (GL reversal, latest defined in migration 094); `inventory.sp_cancel_sales_doc` (`doc_type='salesreturn'` — reverses the posted return's own stock/serial effect, latest defined in migration 121).
**Known gaps or flags:**
- **`sp_reverse_sales_invoice_posting` never got the interstate/IGST logic** that Sales Invoice's forward posting received in migrations 135/166 — it unconditionally posts a CGST+SGST split (`v_half_tax` each), with no `fn_is_interstate_transaction` check and no IGST branch at all. A return against an interstate sale will therefore reverse the wrong tax heads (CGST/SGST instead of IGST), silently misstating both accounts.
- Same `warehouse_id`-only, non-branch-aware stock-reversal pattern as Delivery Challan's cancel path (see DC's Known gaps) — `sp_cancel_sales_doc`'s `salesreturn` branch matches stock-balance rows on `warehouse_id` only, not `branch_id`, so cancelling a posted return tied to a branch-only invoice/return-to-location risks touching the wrong branch's stock row.

## Credit Note

**Business purpose:** The financial settlement document for a Sales Return (or a direct customer adjustment) — price corrections, goodwill credits, short-delivery adjustments — without any stock movement of its own.
**Predecessor / successor documents:** Optionally references a Sales Return (`sales_return_id`/`sales_return_number`) or a Sales Invoice directly (`sales_invoice_id`/`sales_invoice_number`); no successor document.
**Status lifecycle:** `draft → posted → cancelled`.
**Key business rules:** No stock impact by design — the linked Sales Return already restored stock; this note only settles the money side. Free-text `reason` and a `gst_adjustment` boolean flag exist on the header, but no computed GST recalculation happens anywhere in the save path — `gst_pct`/`gst_amount`/`total_amount` on each line are exactly what the frontend sends.
**Accounting/GL impact:** **None, despite the accounting-posting infrastructure existing since migration 091.** `SaveCreditNoteAsync` in `SalesTransactionsDataService.cs` calls only `inventory.sp_save_credit_note` — there is no `accounts.sp_post_credit_note` (or equivalent) anywhere in the codebase, and grepping the migrations for any accounts-schema write keyed to `inv_credit_notes` returns nothing. The original migration's own comment (054) candidly says this was built with "no ledger posting, since no GL subsystem exists anywhere in this codebase" — true at the time it was written, but the GL subsystem has existed since migration 091 and Credit Note was never revisited to use it.
**Frontend:** `creditNoteConfig` → `src/app/inventory/Inventory_Transactions/credit-note/credit-note.ts` (thin shell subclass, 19 lines).
**Backend — tables:** `inventory.inv_credit_notes` (header: `credit_note_number`, `credit_note_date`, `segment_id/name`, `customer_id/name`, `sales_return_id/number`, `sales_invoice_id/number`, `reason`, `gst_adjustment`, `subtotal`, `tax_amount`, `total_amount`, `status`); `inventory.inv_credit_note_items` (line: free-text `description`, `reference`, `amount`, `gst_pct`, `gst_amount`, `total_amount` — a settlement-line shape, not a product/UOM line shape, matching Debit Note's mirrored structure on the purchase side).
**Backend — stored procedures:** `inventory.sp_get_credit_notes`; `inventory.sp_save_credit_note`; `inventory.sp_cancel_sales_doc` (`doc_type='creditnote'` — flips status only, no reversal logic since nothing was ever posted).
**Known gaps or flags:** The single biggest gap in this document: **Credit Note is documentary-only.** Anyone relying on it to actually reduce a customer's ledger balance in Accounts needs to know today it does not — the reduction currently only happens indirectly, via Sales Return's own `accounts.sp_reverse_sales_invoice_posting` call. If Credit Note is ever meant to be the primary settlement trigger (as its own business-purpose copy suggests), a new `accounts.sp_post_credit_note` procedure and C# call site would need to be built from scratch, following the pattern in `07-accounting-integration.md` §3 (branch/warehouse-aware from day one), not the pattern in §2 (hardcoded company/branch).

## Customer Receipt

**Business purpose:** Record money actually received from a customer, allocated against one or more open Sales Invoices (full or partial), reducing the customer's receivable outstanding.
**Predecessor / successor documents:** Requires at least one posted Sales Invoice to allocate against (the allocation multiselect is populated from outstanding invoices). No successor document.
**Status lifecycle:** Implicit via the shared `inv_payment_vouchers` header's own status handling — a receipt is either posted or cancelled (`inventory.sp_cancel_payment_voucher` handles the latter); there is no draft state exposed in this screen's own UI (a receipt is created and posted in one action).
**Key business rules:** No stock impact — purely a financial settlement screen. Not built on the generic `InventoryScreenShell` at all — it is a dedicated, custom component (`payment-receipt-voucher.ts`, 622 lines) that also implements **Vendor Payment** in the same screen via a mode toggle (`mode() === 'pay' ? vendorPaymentConfig : customerReceiptConfig`, both configs pulled from `inventory-screen.model.ts` purely for the guide/help panel copy — the actual form/grid/save logic is bespoke, not shell-driven). TDS auto-apply and TCS-vendor-threshold logic exist as real, tested features on this same component (see its own `.tds-auto-apply.spec.ts`/`.tcs-vendor-threshold.spec.ts`), though those specifically govern the Vendor Payment side of the shared screen.
**Accounting/GL impact:** Yes. `SavePaymentVoucherAsync` (in `InventoryTransactionsDataService.cs`, not `SalesTransactionsDataService.cs`) calls `inventory.sp_save_payment_voucher` first, then — when `voucher_type == 'receipt'` — calls `accounts.sp_post_receipt_voucher` with the voucher number/date/customer name and each payment mode's amount, posting the actual GL entry.
**Frontend:** `customerReceiptConfig` → `src/app/inventory/Inventory_Transactions/payment-receipt-voucher/payment-receipt-voucher.ts` (shared with Vendor Payment; **not** a thin `InventoryScreenShell` subclass — a fully custom component).
**Backend — tables:** `inventory.inv_payment_vouchers` (shared header for both payment and receipt vouchers: `voucher_type`, `voucher_date`, `segment_id/name`, `party_type`, `party_id/name/gstin`, `narration`, `tds_amount`, `tcs_amount`, `tcs_percentage`); `inventory.inv_payment_voucher_allocations` (per-invoice allocation: `invoice_type`, `invoice_id/number`, `allocated_amount`); `inventory.inv_payment_voucher_modes` (per payment-mode split: `mode_key`, `amount`, `ref_json`); `accounts.tbl_trans_total_transactions` (the GL rows from `sp_post_receipt_voucher`).
**Backend — stored procedures:** `inventory.sp_get_payment_vouchers` (filtered by `voucher_type`); `inventory.sp_save_payment_voucher`; `accounts.sp_post_receipt_voucher` (GL posting for the receipt side — introduced migration 090, TDS-adjacent logic layered on in migrations 148–150 for the payment side specifically); `inventory.sp_cancel_payment_voucher`.
**Known gaps or flags:** Being a hand-built component rather than a shell-driven screen means it doesn't automatically inherit shell-wide fixes (branch/warehouse picker unification, reference-picker patterns, etc.) the way the 8 shell-based screens in this document do — any future platform-wide change to those mechanisms needs to be applied here separately, by hand, if it should also apply to Customer Receipt/Vendor Payment.

## POS / Counter Billing

**Business purpose (as designed):** A fast, simplified checkout screen for over-the-counter/walk-in sales — product search, quantity, payment mode, net amount — meant to post as an immediate sale with stock and payment both settled in one action, bypassing the full Enquiry→Quotation→Order→DC→Invoice chain entirely.
**Predecessor / successor documents:** None — designed as a standalone fast path, not a step in the longer chain.
**Status lifecycle:** UI shows `Completed`/`Hold Bill` on its static demo rows only; no real status column or transition exists.
**Key business rules:** None enforced — see Known gaps.
**Accounting/GL impact:** None.
**Frontend:** `posBillingConfig` → `src/app/inventory/Inventory_Transactions/pos-billing/pos-billing.ts` (thin shell subclass, 19 lines — `posMode: 'pos'` in the config switches the shell into its POS-specific layout, but that only affects presentation).
**Backend — tables:** **None.**
**Backend — stored procedures:** **None.** No `SalesTransactionsDataService.cs` (or any other service) method references POS billing in any form; grepping the entire backend for `PosBilling`/`pos_billing` returns zero hits outside the screen-registry route entry.
**Known gaps or flags:** **This is a non-functional stub, identical in nature to Sales Enquiry.** It falls through to the same `default: return;` (load) / `default: this.isSaving.set(false); return;` (save) branches in `inventory-screen-shell.ts`'s central dispatch — the grid always shows the two hardcoded demo POS bills from `inventory-screen.model.ts`, and clicking Save does nothing at all: no network call, no stock decrement, no payment record, no error surfaced to the user. Despite the narrative describing it as bypassing the full document chain for speed, today it doesn't reach any backend at all — a real Sales Invoice created via the **direct-SI path** (no SO, no DC — see the Sales Invoice section above) is, in practice, the only currently-functional fast-checkout equivalent in this codebase.
